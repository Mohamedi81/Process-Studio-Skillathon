Executive Finding

Only 68.2% of cases follow the Signavio happy path (27.9 days); 31.8% incur rework and exception loops taking 36.5 days (+8.6 days longer).

  • Major shadow activities discovered: Post-PO Price Changes (13.6% of cases, +8.8 days delay) and Payment Blocks (2.3% of cases, +11.4 days delay) are not modeled in Signavio.
  • Invoice Receipt -> Payment wait averages 11.7 days (10.6M case-days total), representing the largest throughput bottleneck.
  • Maverick Buying: 77,964 cases (7.0%) record an invoice before the PO item is created in ERP, violating procurement controls.
  • 77,156 PO items (6.9%) end in rejection, deletion, or block due to late demand verification.
